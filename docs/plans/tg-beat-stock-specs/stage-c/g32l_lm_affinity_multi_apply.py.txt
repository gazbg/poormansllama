find_slot (stock placement, g32j only handled ubatches of ONE sequence) puts them into adjacent cells, so both sequences become interleaved cell by cell and no later prompt chunk can take the pass.

Each sequence keeps its own contiguous block while others decode next to it, as long as the gap in front of the next block (lm_block leaves min(16384, size/16) cells) lasts.

## Goal and observable outcome

Make the owner's baseline server command faster on this machine for decode (tok/s with MTP) and for prefill (tok/s, time to first
token), without changing what the model computes. The baseline is the command of `tools/pascal/moe_bench.py` (`BASE`): Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M,
`--hybrid-moe --lm-prefill ... --spec-type draft-mtp ... --spec-mtp-defer`, `-c 262000`, q8_0 KV, one GTX 1070 (8 GiB), i7-4770K (4 cores, 8
threads), 30 GB RAM. Starting point (build `final-bin` / `rem-bin`, remainder off): decode 32.6 / 31.3 / 35.3 tok/s on three chats and 24.8 after a
16k prompt; prefill 471 tok/s at 4k and 574 at 16k.

## Acceptance contract (written before the runs of this goal)

Claims the work may make: speed ratios of one build or setting against a control, on this machine, this model, the harness prompts
(`build/ctx100k/base.txt` cut by tokens; three fixed chats; 256 decode tokens), measured A-B-B-A. No claim about quality beyond bit-identity or
the recorded text-hash comparison; no perplexity claim; no claim for other hardware, models or the Windows build.

Criteria for KEEPING a change (all must hold, decided before seeing results; a change to a criterion is recorded as a decision before the rerun):

- K1 speed: the targeted metric's B mean beats the A mean by at least 2% in an A-B-B-A session, and the worst B arm beats the best A arm
  (the two A arms and the two B arms do not overlap). For decode the metric is ms per verify round (eval time / (tokens - accepted drafts)) when the generated
  text differs between arms, tok/s when it is identical.
- K2 no collateral loss: no other metric (decode tok/s, prefill tok/s at 4k and 16k, peak VRAM) is worse by more than 1% (VRAM: +50 MiB) unless the loss is
  recorded in the decision log as an explicit trade-off with its reason.
- K3 exactness: either bit-identical (the op-level checker finds 0 differences, or the text hashes agree in all arms) or the change is labelled
  TEXT-CHANGING (a different set of resident experts changes the rounding of some experts) and K1 uses ms per round.
- K4 gates (below) pass, and the server logs of the arms show no error, abort, OOM or memory-guard decline that the control does not show.
- K5 documented: `docs/guide/LINUX-PASCAL.md`, `docs/guide/PARAMETERS.md` (if a flag or variable), `docs/currentstate.md` and the ledger below.

Default rule: a change is on by default only if it is exact (K3 without the label), K1 holds in at least two independent sessions or at two prompt lengths,
and it cannot affect another GPU architecture (gated by an architecture check). Otherwise it is an opt-in flag or variable, or only advice.

Gates (commands run from the worktree root; expected result):

- G1 `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 build/llama-cuda/bin/test-backend-ops test -o MUL_MAT_ID -b CUDA0` and `-o MUL_MAT`: all pass (887 / 1,218 at the start; more with new cases). Once per remainder mode when `GGML_CUDA_MMQ_REMAINDER` is touched.
- G2 `build/llama-cuda/bin/test-moe-lean-expert`: 0 failures (3,295 checks at the start), after any change to `llama-moe-lean.h`.
- G3 `build/pascal/check_mmq_remainder`: 0 comparisons differ (30 at the start), after any change to the MMQ launch.
- G4 `tools/pascal/abba.sh TAG A_BIN B_BIN --pp 4096,16384 --deep 16384 --chats 3 --tg 256 --hlog` with `ARGS_A` / `ARGS_B` for per-arm options; the table of `abba_table.py` is the evidence.
- G5 the GPU is free before every arm (`moe_bench.py` refuses to start otherwise); GPU 0 only; no build, test or host analysis while an arm runs.

Stop condition (the goal is accepted when): every hypothesis of the ledger is resolved (KEPT, REJECTED with evidence, or BLOCKED by a never-autonomous action
with what is lost), AND a closing profile of decode and of prefill (nsys, as in `tools/pascal/README.md`) shows no kernel category of 5% or more of its phase
without a resolved hypothesis. If the closing profile exposes one, it becomes a ledger entry and the work continues.

Validation of the closing profile: `nsys profile` of one decode arm and one prefill arm through `moe_bench.py --wrap`, tables by `tools/dense/nsys-prefill.py` and the decode script in this file's history (currentstate 2026-10-24).

## Method

Screening: single arms of `moe_bench.py` (`--pp "" --deep 16384 --chats 3 --tg 256 --hlog`, side-copied binaries via `--bin`) queued one after the other, with a control arm first and
last in the queue (drift check), compared by `tools/pascal/arms_table.py`. A screening result under 2% is noise (the control spread is 0.1 to 1%). A candidate that passes screening goes to a G4 session.

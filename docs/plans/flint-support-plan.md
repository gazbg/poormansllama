## 3. Ceiling arithmetic from the T-FL-00 measurements (NOT a measurement of the pass)

Measured inputs (section 11): the crowded stock prefill of the 8,604-token probe takes 11.2 s. The GPU runs kernels for 1.42 s of it (about 6,000 tok/s of pure GPU work) and the copy engine moves 123 GiB at 14.4 GB/s for 9.17 s (about five full sweeps of the 25.9 GiB of banks, one per 2,048-token ubatch). Copies and kernels never overlap.

A layer-major pass reads every bank once per batch (up to 16,384 tokens) and can overlap that copy with compute. For one 8.6k batch: copy about 25.9 GiB / 14.4 GB/s = 1.9 s, kernels about 1.4 s, overlapped: about 2.0 s, a ceiling near 4,300 tok/s (copy-bound at this size). Against the best stock arms of section 11 that is about 2.2x the uncrowded arm (1,985), about 5.5x the loose crowded arm (784) and about 9x the strict crowded arm (464). From roughly 12k tokens per batch the pass is compute-bound, and at 50k the kernel time (and the attention part of it, not measured here) sets the rate.

This is a ceiling, not a prediction: it ignores the pass's own overheads (the Qwen MoE pass measured 1.0-1.4x over the window at 2k-4k tokens and 2.3x at 24k-50k, `AGENTS.md` G32), attention growth at depth, and the cost of the explicit masks (P4). The first version of this section inferred a compute rate of about 3,400 tok/s from the uncrowded stock arm; the crowded profile measures about 6,000 tok/s of kernel work, so that inference is withdrawn (the uncrowded arm was not profiled).

## 5. Design (PROPOSED; each is a decision the owner can overrule)

- **P1 Packages.** One pageable package per MoE layer = the whole routed-expert bank (gate, up, down; SDD section 4). 47 packages. Static, resident, not paged: attention, shared experts, router, norms, the two dense layers, embedding, output, KV. Slot size = the largest bank, layer 14's 692 MiB (the other 46 are 562 MiB; found by T-FL-00); 2 slots (1.35 GiB).
- **P2 One graph body.** Extract flint's per-layer attention and FFN from the `graph` constructor into members (`build_layer_attn`, `build_layer_ffn`, as `qwen35moe.cpp` has), used by both the stock constructor and the pass's piece builder, so routing and arithmetic are one piece of code. The extraction must leave the stock path bit-identical (FL-4).
- **P3 Memory.** Generalise the pass from `llama_memory_hybrid` to a small interface with two implementations: the existing hybrid one, untouched in behaviour, and a `llama_kv_cache_iswa` one (base + window). Rejected as default: `--swa-full` (about 9.3 GiB extra at 262k breaks the crowded case); kept as a fallback for small contexts.
- **P4 Attention in the pass.** Full-attention layers (14) take the implicit causal mask, made per-layer (`hparams.is_swa(il)` instead of the model-wide `swa_type` test). Window layers (35) take an explicit window mask built once per chunk and shared by all 35 layers (about 2,048 x 2,600 f16 = 10 MiB). Sinks are passed through to the flash-attention op.
- **P5 Phase order.** Phase 1 (prefill) first (charter). Phase 2 (hybrid decode) only after T-FL-00 shows decode headroom in the crowded case; uncrowded stock decode is already 48-56 tok/s.
- **P6 Server scope.** `-np 1`, no `--lm-prefill-fuse`, `-prearm`, `-canonical`, no second GPU. Those need window-cache-aware checkpoints (U7) and are a separate plan.

## 6. Contract FL (written before the first gate run; a change is a dated decision with its reason, made before the rerun)

**Definitions.** STOCK = the same build, no fork flags, `-fit` default, `-lm none`, q8_0 KV, fa on, `-np 1 -t 16`, ubatch chosen from the recorded grid (UNCROWDED: best of 2048 / 4096; CROWDED: the best arm that meets the ceiling below). LM = the same build with `--lm-prefill`. A "cold prompt" = `cache_prompt:false`, repo text with a needle (`tools/dense_probe.py`), the server's own `prompt_per_second`. CROWDED = the owner's keep-free rule, 16,000 MiB free in the CUDA-runtime view, which on this machine is a poller peak of at most about 7,376 MiB (the runtime view reads about 1,080 MiB above the poller; AGENTS.md), with every layer and all KV on the GPU, for the stock arm and for the LM arm alike (D-FL-2: the stock auto-fit at `-fitt 16000` starves and is UNFIT). Peak VRAM = the external poller's maximum (`vram.csv`), never the runner's own field (AGENTS.md gotchas).

- **FL-0 go / no-go (T-FL-00).** Proceed to T-FL-01 only if, in the CROWDED stock arm at the 8,604-token probe, host-to-device copy time is >= 25% of the prefill wall in the nsys profile (ceiling >= 1.33x). Otherwise Phase 1 stops and the result is reported. (The 25% bar is unchanged; the arm identity was fixed by D-FL-2 after the matrix and before any LM run; the profiled arm, ubatch 2048, passes with a wide margin, section 11.)
- **FL-1 support.** `flint` starts with `--lm-prefill`, the log says the pass is enabled for `flint`, `LLAMA_LM_LOG=1` shows the pass ran, and needle prompts of about 4k and 24k tokens are answered (substring `7391`); 50k is a manual owner-invoked run. Unsupported setups are refused loudly with the cause named: a mixed K/V cache type pair, a window cache smaller than `n_swa` + chunk, no CUDA, no flash attention.
- **FL-2 equivalence.** At an EQUAL chunk shape (`--lm-prefill-ubatch 512` against STOCK `-ub 512`), over the 8 prompts of `tools/dense_equiv.py` plus its 24-prompt KL set: the first generated token is the same token, its logprob differs by <= 0.07 nats, and the 24-token greedy continuation is identical or first differs where the reference's top-2 gap is <= 0.25 nats. - **FL-3 prefill speed.** Cold-prompt rate of LM at the 8,604-token probe and at about 50k tokens >= 1.15x the best STOCK arm of the same session, UNCROWDED and CROWDED separately (the CROWDED baseline is the strict arm of D-FL-2; the ratio to the loose arm is reported beside it); and CROWDED LM >= 0.90x UNCROWDED LM at the same prompt (precedent: DQ-3, DQ-4). The speed claim is made per configuration measured, nothing else.
- **FL-4 default-off identity.** Without the new flags: the first-token logprobs and 64-token greedy text of two flint prompts through the stock path are bit-identical to the pre-change build (side copy of the old `llama.dll` and `ggml-cuda.dll`); the Qwen3.6 MoE and dense Qwen3.8 `--lm-prefill` smokes still start and answer; `test-backend-ops` suites `FLASH_ATTN_EXT`, `MUL_MAT`, `MUL_MAT_ID` pass; the host ctest set passes.
- **FL-5 hybrid decode (Phase 2).** Routed expert ids and gate weights are bit-identical to the stock graph for a recorded 64-token series per prompt; decode >= 1.10x the STOCK split at the same VRAM budget in 3 repeated runs, otherwise the result is reported as measured with the root cause.
- **FL-6 honesty.** All claims state prompt size, flags, and the arms they were measured against; nothing extends to other models, quantisations, GPUs, `-np > 1`, or contexts not run. Gates are soft evidence; acceptance is the owner's.

- **FL-7 Qwen non-regression (the owner's `/goal` condition: "without impeding performance of Qwen"; written 2026-11-02 before any engine change).** CONTROL = `build/llama-cuda/bin-ctl-qwen/`, a side copy of the binaries built from tree 99fa7226 and taken before the first engine edit (AGENTS.md side-copy rule; llama.dll sha256 B0F9F84C1E2582CF, ggml-cuda.dll B04485AFF86146E5). - **D-FL-1 (2026-11-02; T-FL-01 answered by reading the code, then confirmed by the pass itself)** How does the pass run on the window cache? The pass applies every chunk's memory context before running any layer (so each chunk's KV cells and inputs are fixed up front) and forces the attention cache's cells to `c0 + position`. For flint's base cache (14 full-attention layers, `n_swa` 0) that holds unchanged and the implicit causal mask works. The window cache (35 layers) is a ring of `n_swa + n_ubatch` cells (2,816 at `-ub 2048`): applying all chunks first leaves its cell metadata in the END state, and the stock mask fill (`llama_kv_cache::set_input_kq_mask`) reads the CURRENT metadata, so any later-built mask of an early chunk is wrong. The K/V DATA is not a problem (a layer processes its chunks in ascending order, and a cell is only overwritten by a chunk that no later chunk of that layer needs). Options: (a) replay the apply per layer (35 times the host cost); (b) fill each chunk's window mask once, right after that chunk's `apply()` while the metadata is exactly the stock state, upload it to a persistent per-chunk device buffer (11.5 MB per chunk at ubatch 2048, about 100 MB per segment), and let all 35 window layers read it; (c) a pass-private linear K/V buffer plus a flush into the ring (rejected: it duplicates the attention-cache write path). Chosen (b). The mask is produced by the unmodified stock function, so it is the stock mask by construction. The base cache keeps `lm_force` and the implicit causal mask, per layer (`!hparams.is_swa(il)`). Needs `-ub` >= the pass chunk (the ring must hold `n_swa` + chunk cells); the enable gate refuses otherwise with the cause named. The pass's memory access is abstracted behind one small helper (hybrid: the attention + recurrent caches, as now; iswa: base + window caches, no recurrent state), and the hybrid branches keep their exact behaviour. ## 12. Owner summary (the agent's autonomous acceptance; not the owner's approval)

**1. Goal.** `/goal`: implement `docs/plans/flint-support-plan.md` without impeding Qwen. Hypothesis: the layer-major prefill that already speeds up the Qwen MoE should speed up prompt processing of flint too: flint's ~40 expert banks do not fit beside the rest of the model in 24 GB, and the stock path copies them for every batch (T-FL-00: 81.8% of the prefill wall is host-to-device copy).

**2. How it works, plainly.** A long prompt is cut into chunks. The stock path runs all 49 layers over one chunk, then the next, so every chunk copies every expert bank again. The pass reverses the loops: it loads layer 0's bank once, runs every chunk through layer 0 in position order, keeps the activations in a buffer, then loads layer 1's bank, and so on. The KV cache and the window cache stay where the stock path keeps them, so later requests reuse them. Flint's window layers look back 513 positions only, so their cache is a ring; the pass fills each chunk's window mask once, while the ring still holds that chunk's history. Bank copies go in small pieces so a small input copy is not queued behind a 432 MB bank.

**3. Glossary.** PP: prompt processing (prefill), in tokens per second. TG: token generation (decode). Bank: one layer's routed-expert weights (about 0.5 to 0.7 GB for flint). Slot: a VRAM buffer holding one bank (the pass uses two). Slab: the activation buffer between layers. Chunk: a slice of the prompt processed together (2,048 tokens here). Segment: chunks that share one slab. KV cache: keys and values per position. Window cache / ring: the 513-position cache of the sliding-window layers. Sinks: flint's per-head attention term. Crowded: the card full (experts on the host, `--cpu-moe`). Uncrowded: experts partly on the card. Keep-free ceiling 7,376 MiB: the owner's rule for the poller's peak (16,000 MiB free in the CUDA view). Lazy KV: the KV cache's VRAM is committed on demand. Paced copies: bank copies in 2 MiB pieces, at most 2 in flight. Equal shape: the same chunk size on both sides (stock output also moves with its own chunk size, D-FL-8). KL: Kullback-Leibler divergence of the first-token distribution over the top-20 tokens. Logprob: log of a token's probability. Poller: the nvidia-smi sampler, the unit of every peak. PCIe: the bus between CPU and GPU.

**4. Pros and cons (measured / expected / unproven; evidence in section 11).**
- *Measured:* flint prompt processing 2.0x (8.6k) and 1.8x (50.7k) faster than the best stock uncrowded arm, and 7.3x faster than the strict crowded stock baseline, inside the 7,376 MiB ceiling (FL-3). Equal-shape outputs match stock on the device-matched reference: 8 of 8 prompts at each shape, KL mean 0.0005 at 512 and 0.0003 at 2048 (FL-2). Qwen V1 and V2 unchanged within 0.3% on prompt speed and decode, same VRAM, identical outputs in the tested configurations (FL-7). The 50.7k needle is found (FL-1).
- *Measured, with a limit:* the crowded ceiling needs the lazily committed KV, enabled by an environment variable (D-FL-12); without it the crowded pass peaks at 9,671 MiB.
- *Measured, NOT met:* crowded pass at 0.872 of the uncrowded pass at 8.6k (0.882 with `--lm-prefill-slots 3`; was 0.84 before D-FL-24) against the 0.90 bar (FL-3 clause; 0.92 at 50.7k, met). The pass is copy-bound at the PCIe floor; section 11, FL-3 round 2. Against the original FL-2 bar of 0.07 one prompt exceeds it (0.1036, inside the stock path's own noise floor of 0.1105); accepted under the owner's noise-floor rule (D-FL-23).
- *Expected, unproven:* the paced-copy gain at other prompt lengths and on other GPUs; any gain at 100k to 250k prompts (speed was measured to 50.7k only).
- *Measured:* flint decode with `--hybrid-moe` is 2.4x to 3.7x (8.6k) and 2.1x to 5.0x (50.7k) the stock decode at the same VRAM budget; ids and gate weights equal the stock router (FL-5). The text of a hybrid run differs from a stock run (host rounding, D-FL-21).
- *Unproven:* quality beyond the needle questions and the equal-shape logprobs; the hybrid decode on contexts above 50.7k; `-np > 1`; other flint checkpoints.
- *Costs:* while it runs, the pass holds two bank slots and a slab in VRAM (the expert cache shrinks for it), and it refuses a layer whose experts are split between device and host (D-FL-16).
